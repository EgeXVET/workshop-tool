      Commercial terms
      
      
      The terms as offered. Every line can be edited, removed or added to during the meeting —

        Live editing. Click any line to change the wording. Use × to drop a point and

      The principle behind the terms
      
        The value was established before the price. The terms are built so that the first cycle is safe for both
        sides — limited exposure, full support, and a clear review point before the volume grows.

      Commercial meeting · Step 4
      
      
      Your decision — within one week
      
      
      Everything needed for the decision is now on the table. We do not ask for an answer today.
        Take the week, review it internally, and come back with a decision you are sure about.
      

      
      
        
      Why we give you the week
      
        
      After three meetings you have the complete picture: the products and the evidence behind them, the
          technical programme for your production systems, the registration route and timeline, the commercial
          terms and the first year plan. A decision made on that basis holds. One made under pressure in a meeting
          does not, and neither of us benefits from that.
      
      
      

      
      
        
      
          
      What you now have from us
