Care today for a better tomorrow

Antimicrobial resistance is a growing pressure on animal production and on consumer health. XVET develops alternative and responsible solutions for less-antibiotics animal production — drinking water additives and feed additives that support performance without relying on routine antibiotic use.

That positioning is what a distributor sells against the market: not another commodity additive, but a documented alternative in a category that regulators and buyers are moving towards.

Health — alternative solutions Feed — hygiene, efficiency, performance Farm — farm-level challenges

How we proceed

The agreed process from here.

1

Directly after this workshop: brochures and technical documentation for the selected products, together with the price offer.

2

Feedback within two weeks

Confirmation of which of the presented products are relevant for the market, so the focus can be narrowed before the commercial discussion.

3

First order quantities per product

Once the products are chosen, the intended first order quantities define packaging, production planning and shipment consolidation.

Technical support

Direct line to Hamburg
