    Who is XVET

    XVET is a German, GMP+ certified company with more than 24 years of experience developing and exporting non-pharmaceutical feed and drinking water solutions for livestock production.

    Our goal goes beyond supplying products. We work as a long-term market development partner to our distributors, combining high-quality German-manufactured solutions with technical expertise, regulatory guidance, and localized marketing support.
