    How we proceed

    The agreed process from here.

    1

    XVET sends the product information and the price offer

    Directly after this workshop: brochures and technical documentation for the selected products, together with the price offer.

    2

    Feedback within two weeks

    Confirmation of which of the presented products are relevant for the market, so the focus can be narrowed before the commercial discussion.

    3

    First order quantities per product

    Once the products are chosen, the intended first order quantities define packaging, production planning and shipment consolidation.

    4

    Proforma invoice and registration

    XVET issues the proforma invoice and prepares the registration dossier for the destination market. First shipment follows once registration is completed.
